What this page does not tell you

That this model is safe, or that it is interchangeable with any other model in this archive. Two models can share tensor shapes and slice widths while interpreting those numbers completely differently — column meanings inside a slice, MDN field ordering, and input normalization are all invisible here. Qualification is the fork's job. See Integrate.

This registry asserts blob identity and upstream provenance. It does not assert that a model is safe to drive, or that two models are interchangeable.
